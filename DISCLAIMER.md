# Important notices — RMTI Sandbox (Oncology & Public Health Surveillance)

This file states plainly what the software is for and what it is not for. It is a statement of intended use, **not legal advice**.

## 1. Intended use

This software is provided for teaching, methodological demonstration and academic research on the Risk Mechanism Theory Index (RMTI).

## 2. Not a medical device

- It has not been reviewed, cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any other regulator.
- It has not been validated as clinical decision-support software.
- It is not intended to diagnose, treat, monitor, or make decisions about an individual patient.
- Its scores are relative, rubric-based indices. They are **not** probabilities that a person will develop cancer and are not predictions for individuals.
- The values in the worked examples are illustrative.

## 3. How to read the messages on screen

Statements about surveillance intensity, work-up or referral are *framework readings*: they show what the research framework would flag for discussion by a clinical team. They are not recommendations, orders or instructions for any patient, and they cannot judge urgency.

## 4. Clinical judgment stays with clinicians

Any decision about surveillance, diagnosis, treatment or referral for a real patient must be made by qualified healthcare professionals using current clinical guidelines, complete diagnostic information and their own professional judgment. Nothing shown by this tool should be used in place of that.

## 5. Third-party clinical instruments

The tool refers to published instruments (for example aMAP, THRI, ALBI and the BCLC staging system). They belong to their original authors and should be used as published. This project does not validate, modify or replace them.

## 6. Research status

The HCC / oncology application of RMTI is the subject of work in preparation that has not yet completed peer review. Until it is published, treat the oncology domain as a research prototype.

## 7. No patient data

The tool runs in your browser and sends nothing to a server, but please do not type patient names or any identifying information into it.

## 8. No warranty

The software is provided "as is" under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`), without warranty of any kind and without liability for its use.

## 9. If you are considering clinical use

Whether software counts as a regulated medical device or clinical decision-support tool depends on its intended use and on the jurisdiction, and software that produces a score for an individual patient can fall inside those rules. Any move toward use in patient care would need clinical validation, institutional review and regulatory advice first. That is outside the scope of this repository, and this version is not offered for that purpose.
